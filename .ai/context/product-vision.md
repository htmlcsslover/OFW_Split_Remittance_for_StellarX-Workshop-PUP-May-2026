# Product Vision: CareFund Stellar

CareFund Stellar is a transparent social impact funding platform powered by Stellar.

## Core Purpose
It enables:
- **Donors** to fund verified causes.
- **Beneficiaries** to request funding.
- **Organizations** to distribute aid and manage programs.
- **Admins** to verify and approve payouts and users.

## Unified Marketplace
The platform focuses on transparency, verification, and public accountability. It operates as a single marketplace supporting diverse impact categories as data attributes, not separate features:
- **MENTAL_HEALTH**: Therapy and counseling support.
- **SCHOOL_LUNCH**: Free lunch programs for students.
- **SENIOR_CARE**: Direct stipends and care for the elderly.
- **SOLAR_PROJECT**: Community renewable energy infrastructure.

## MVP Roles (Simplified)
1. **DONOR**: Contributes USDC/XLM to funds.
2. **BENEFICIARY**: The ultimate recipient of support (memos/data).
3. **ORGANIZATION**: NGOs, Schools, or Clinics that manage the fund execution.
4. **ADMIN**: Platform governors who verify organizations and approve payouts.
