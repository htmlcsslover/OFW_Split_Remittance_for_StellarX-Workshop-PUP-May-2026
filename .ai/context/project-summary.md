# Project Summary: BayanFund-StellarX

## Overview
BayanFund-StellarX is a community-driven crowdfunding platform built on the Stellar network. It leverages Soroban smart contracts to ensure transparency, security, and automated fund distribution for community projects.

## Problem Statement
Traditional crowdfunding often lacks transparency in fund management and distribution. In community settings (like "Bayanihan" in the Philippines), trust is paramount, but hard to verify at scale.

## Solution
BayanFund uses Stellar's fast and low-cost network to enable micro-donations and Soroban contracts to:
1. Lock funds until specific milestones are met.
2. Enable community voting on fund releases.
3. Provide an immutable audit trail of all contributions and disbursements.

## Target Audience
- Local communities looking to fund grassroots projects.
- Individual donors seeking high transparency.
- Social impact organizations.

## Key Features (MVP)
- **Campaign Creation**: Users can start crowdfunding campaigns with defined goals.
- **Stellar Wallet Integration**: Seamless connection with wallets like Freighter.
- **Milestone-Based Funding**: Funds are held in escrow and released upon milestone completion.
- **Real-Time Transparency**: A dashboard showing live fund status and transaction history.
